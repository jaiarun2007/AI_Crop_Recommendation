## Project Objective
An AI-powered smart farming system that helps farmers make data-driven decisions, combining
crop recommendation, disease detection, and yield prediction in one platform.

## Problems Addressed
Incorrect crop selection (poor yields), late disease detection (pest/disease spread), excessive
fertilizer use (cost + soil harm).

## AI Solution Inputs
Soil data (N, P, K, pH), climate data (temperature, humidity, rainfall, seasonal patterns via
weather APIs), plant leaf images (CNN-based disease diagnosis), historical farming data (past
yields and trends).

## System Architecture — Three Core AI Components
- Crop Recommendation Model: classification model suggesting best crops given soil/climate.
- Disease Detection Model: CNN/YOLO-based model identifying plant diseases from leaf images and
  suggesting remedies.
- Yield Prediction Model: regression model estimating expected crop yield before harvest.

## Technology Stack & Data Sources
Python, PyTorch/TensorFlow, OpenCV for image processing, a Flutter mobile app (Firebase/REST
APIs) for delivery to farmers. Data sources: government Soil Health Card data, public weather
APIs, agricultural datasets, PlantVillage leaf image dataset.

## Expected Benefits
Higher crop yields, reduced fertilizer waste, early disease prevention, simple AI-driven
decision support (including offline scenarios), promotion of sustainable farming practices.

## One-Line Summary
"An AI-driven smart farming assistant that recommends the right crop, detects plant diseases
early, and predicts yield using soil, weather, and image data."
