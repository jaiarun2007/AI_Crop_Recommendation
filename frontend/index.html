🌾 AI-Based Crop Recommendation & Climate-Adaptive Farming Assistant

Demo prototype — crop, disease, yield, weather & fertilizer intelligence

1. Crop Recommendation

Enter soil nutrients and climate readings to get the top recommended crops.

2. Leaf Disease Screening

Upload a leaf photo to screen for visible discoloration/lesions.

3. Yield Prediction

Estimate expected yield for a crop/region/year given rainfall, pesticide use, and temperature.

4. Weather Intelligence

Live forecast + rule-based agro-alerts (heatwave, heavy rain, high wind, dry spell) from Open-Meteo.

5. Fertilizer Recommendation

N-P-K balancing against ideal crop nutrient targets, with real fertilizer-product dosing.

6. Irrigation Schedule

FAO-56 crop-coefficient method (ETc = ET0 × Kc) against live rainfall forecast.

Backend: FastAPI · Crop & yield models: trained on real public datasets (NPK/climate + FAO yield data) · Disease screening: OpenCV colour-heuristic baseline · Weather: live Open-Meteo forecasts · Fertilizer: rule-based N-P-K balancing · Irrigation: FAO-56 crop-coefficient method
